About & Inquiries

I am a multidisciplinary artist working across fine art photography, painting, drawing, and literature. My practice resists categorization: visually eclectic and intellectually driven, my writing moves fluidly between poetry, essays, short fiction, and philosophical and scientific inquiry.

As a self-taught creator with the spirit of an Enlightenment polymath, I am guided by an uncompromising pursuit of the unfamiliar. My work emerges from a deep fascination with the rare, the unconventional, and the non-standard—an exploration of creativity unbound by norms.
